Great question — let’s break down your **humanoid control space**.

---

### 🔧 **1. Revolute Joints (4 joints total)**

| Joint       | Range (radians) |
| ----------- | --------------- |
| Left Elbow  | \[0, π]         |
| Right Elbow | \[0, π]         |
| Left Knee   | \[-π, 0]        |
| Right Knee  | \[-π, 0]        |

🧠 **So your revolute control space is:**

$$
\mathcal{U}_{\text{rev}} \subset \mathbb{R}^4 \quad \text{with} \quad u_i \in 
\begin{cases}
[0, \pi], & \text{for elbows} \\
[-\pi, 0], & \text{for knees}
\end{cases}
$$

---

### 🎯 **2. Spherical Joints (4 joints × 3 DOF each = 12 params)**

Your spherical joints (hips and ankles) use **Euler angles**: roll, pitch, yaw.

For each spherical joint:

$$
\text{spherical}_i = [\text{roll}_i, \text{pitch}_i, \text{yaw}_i]
$$

These are usually unconstrained in theory, but in practice:

* **Safe operating bounds:** $[-0.5, 0.5]$ radians for each axis is a good start.
* If using `setJointMotorControlMultiDof`, clip roll/pitch/yaw before converting to quaternion.

🧠 **So your spherical control space is:**

$$
\mathcal{U}_{\text{sph}} \subset \mathbb{R}^{12} \quad \text{with} \quad \text{roll/pitch/yaw} \in [-0.5, 0.5]
$$

---

### 📐 **Total Control Space**

| Joint Type | DOF | Dimension | Bounds                                    |
| ---------- | --- | --------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Revolute   | 1   | 4         | \[0, π] and \[−π, 0]                      |
| Spherical  | 3   | 12        | Typically \[−0.5, 0.5] for roll/pitch/yaw |
| **Total**  | —   | **16**    | Combined 16-dimensional control vector    |

### 🧩 **How to Represent Actions**

If you're using **center\_sampling**, define:

```python
action_center = [default values for 16 DOF]  # standing pose
action = action_center + small_deltas
```

If you're using **increment**, then actions are deltas added on top of current joint values:
